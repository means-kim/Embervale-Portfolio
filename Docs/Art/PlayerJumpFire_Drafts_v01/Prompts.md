## 1

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE isolated 2D side-view FIRE JUMP TAKEOFF EFFECT sprite, a concept draft for a hand-painted dark-fantasy metroidvania. References: first image is ONLY movement composition: burst spreading left and right from the feet with diagonally rising tapered streaks, do not copy the character, scenery or black annotation. Second reference is the actual game protagonist and dictates the painterly orange/crimson fire shapes, buttery pale-yellow cores and restrained dark red contour. Third reference dictates existing warm blade-like VFX highlights. Render ONLY the effect, no character, floor, UI, lettering or labels. True transparent background, no checkerboard painted into image. Wide horizontal effect, single full uncropped silhouette, ample transparent margins. Stylized clean flowing pointed flame tongues, bold readable hand-painted shapes and a few embers, limited soft glow, no photoreal fire, no pixel art, no dense smoke, no giant explosion. Strongest light at bottom center launch point; sides fan outward/upward; open center above base so player remains readable. This is a single peak animation pose, not a spritesheet.
Variant 1: faithful compact twin fan. Two balanced low flame jets flick sharply outward left and right, thin ivory-yellow tapered streak cores and small curled orange flame tips. Low central ignition at feet, about three body widths wide but under half a body height, lean and quick.
